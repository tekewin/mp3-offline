# MP3 Offline: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

MP3 Offline is a music player that works entirely on your device.

**What the app accesses.** With your permission, the app reads audio files in your device's Music folder so it can list and play them. It reads the song title, artist, album, duration and any cover art stored inside the files.

**What the app stores.** Your playlists and your light/dark theme choice are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They are not backed up or transferred by the app.

**What the app collects or shares.** Nothing. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. No information leaves your device.

**Deleting your data.** Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes your playlists and settings. Your music files are never modified or deleted by the app.
